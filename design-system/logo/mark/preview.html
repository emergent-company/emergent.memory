The letter system

Same lattice, same rules. The wordmark is drawn from this set, never typed as live text.

· Orthogonal only: no curves, no radii, square terminals.
· A segment may be long, never thicker than 1u — the gate rejects any filled 2×2 block.
· The inner form is inset 1u from the cap line and stops short of the baseline, so a letter's feet are its outer stems.
· M and W are the only wide letters; every other box is 4u.
